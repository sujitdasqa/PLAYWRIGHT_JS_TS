## Tips

1. **Customize shortcuts**: Go to `File > Preferences > Keyboard Shortcuts` or press `Ctrl + K` `Ctrl + S`.
2. **Chord shortcuts**: Some shortcuts use two key combinations (e.g., `Ctrl + K` then `Ctrl + S`). Press the first chord, release, then press the second.
3. **Keybinding JSON**: For advanced customization, open `keybindings.json` from the Keyboard Shortcuts editor.
